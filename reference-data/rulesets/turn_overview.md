Orden de llamada (guía por fases)

Preparation phase (once per session):
- Character cards are dealt.
- Buildings are dealt. (only if buildings are in play).
- Village is separated into two groups for the Prejudiced Manipulator.
- Gypsy’s cards.(only if Gypsy is in play).
- Town Crier’s cards. (only if Town Crier is in play).
- Thief’s 2 extra cards.
- Actor’s 3 cards.(all visible, no werevolves cards).
- Sheriff’s Election (can be later at moderator's discretion).

Character call order:
Characters called on the first night:
- The Thief. (look at the 2 cards and they may change)
- The Actor. (choose whether to use one of the 3 cards powers)
- Cupid. (choose lovers)
- The Seer.
- The Fox.
- The Lovers. (recognize each other)
- The Wandering Judge (choose signal).
- The Two Sisters.
- The Three Brothers.
- The Wild Child. (choose role model)
- The Bear Tamer. (grunt).
- The Scandalmonger.
- The Pyromaniac.
- The Defender.
- All of the Werewolves, including the Wolf-Hound if they've chosen to be a Werewolf, the White Werewolf, the Cursed Wolf‑Father, and the Big Bad Wolf. The Little Girl can spy.
- The Baker opens and then quickly closes their eyes. (if baker is in play).
- The Cursed Wolf-Father.
- The Big Bad Wolf.
- The Witch.
- The Gypsy (can choose a Medium).
- The Piper.
- Charmed players.

Each night:
- The Actor. (if there are any cards left).
- The Seer.
- The Fox.
- The Two Sisters. (every other night).
- The Three Brothers. (every other night).
- The Scandalmonger.
- The Pyromaniac.
- The Defender.
- All of the Werewolves, including the Wolf-Hound if their choice was to be a Werewolf, the Wild Child if their role model is eliminated, and the player who may be infected by the Cursed Wolf‑Father. The Little Girl can spy.
- The Baker opens and then quickly closes their eyes.
- The White Werewolf (every other night).
- The Cursed Wolf-Father.
- The Big Bad Wolf (as long as no Werewolf has been eliminated).
- The Witch.
- The Gypsy (can choose a Medium).
- The Piper.
- Charmed players.

Each day:
- Victims are revealed and resolve effects upon death.
- Bear’s grunt.
- Medium (chosen by the Gypsy).
- The Town Crier.
- Debate.
- Vote (then check for the Devoted Servant's ability).
- Angel wins (only if eliminated on the first vote).
- Possible second vote (if the Wandering Judge so wishes) then check for the Devoted Servant's ability.

Start over at the “each night“ step, etc.

Notes
- Single-call roles (Thief, Wolfdog, Wild Child, Cupid, Brothers/Sisters) do not repeat except for optional re-awakens (Brothers/Sisters) or status changes (Wild Child, Wolfdog).
- Adjust the exact order to avoid accidental clues and according to active modules (buildings, Town Crier, Baker, etc.).
