# Conditions of Victory / Defeat

This summary consolidates common end-state conditions for the Village Storyteller “Werewolf” sessions. Use it to decide when the game can be finished.

## Village team wins
- All Werewolves (including Big Bad Wolf, White Werewolf, Cursed Wolf-Father, Wolf-Hound if turned, etc.) are eliminated.
- No hostile special faction remains that overrides village victory (Piper charmed win not fulfilled, lovers dual win not active).

## Werewolves win
- Living Werewolves **≥** living non‑Werewolves, or only Werewolves remain.
- Any lone hostile (Piper, Lovers) that could contest this is eliminated or already resolved.

## Lovers win (dual or solo)
- Both Lovers remain alive and **all other players are eliminated**. Lovers share a joint victory even if from different factions.

## Piper wins
- All surviving players are **Charmed** by the Piper.
- If Piper dies before charming everyone, this condition cannot be met.

## Angel instant win
- If the Angel is eliminated during the very first day’s vote or the first night, the Angel wins immediately and the game ends.

## Draw / cancellation (optional moderator call)
- A moderator may declare a stalemate or cancellation if no victory path remains reachable (e.g., soft lock).

> Note: Some variants introduce buildings, Town Crier, Medium, or other modules; they do not override the core win checks above unless explicitly stated in their rules.***
